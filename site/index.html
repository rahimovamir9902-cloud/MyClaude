05 — Straight answers

The things owners actually ask.

Will it sound like a robot?

Not if it's built properly. We write it in your voice, using the phrases your staff already use, and you approve every standard message before it goes live. When a conversation gets unusual, it stops guessing and passes it to you with the full history.

What happens when it doesn't know something?

It says so, takes the customer's contact, and alerts you — it never invents an answer about your prices or availability. Then we add that case, so the same gap doesn't happen twice.

Do I have to change my number or my tools?

No. It sits on top of the number, accounts and calendar you already use. If you decide to stop, everything stays yours — the number, the customer list, the conversation history.

Which languages does it handle?

Russian, Tajik and English out of the box, and it answers in whichever language the customer wrote in. Other languages are possible — ask on the call.

How quickly can it be live?

Most builds go live within a week of the audit. The slow part is never the technology — it's getting your prices, rules and edge cases written down clearly, and we do that part with you.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

The monthly is month to month — stop whenever you like. If the first month doesn't bring in more booked work than it costs, tell us and we'll either fix it or refund the month.
